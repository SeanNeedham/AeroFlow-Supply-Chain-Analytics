# AeroFlow Components — Supply Chain Performance & Inventory Optimisation

## Project Overview

AeroFlow Components is a multi-site aerospace parts distributor managing 300 components across six operational sites and a network of suppliers. The business relies on accurate demand forecasting, appropriate inventory levels and reliable supplier performance to maintain product availability.

## Business Problem

Increasing backorders, supplier delivery variability and forecast errors have raised concerns around product availability and inventory efficiency. Management requires a centralised analytical solution to identify supply-chain risks and prioritise corrective action.

## Business Objectives

- Improve visibility of inventory and product availability.

- Evaluate supplier delivery and quality performance.
- Assess demand forecast accuracy.
- Identify parts exposed to supply-chain risk.
- Provide management with actionable recommendations.

## Business Questions

1. Which parts and sites are most exposed to inventory shortages and backorders?

2. Which suppliers have the greatest problems with late and incomplete deliveries?
3. How accurately does forecast demand predict actual consumption?
4. Which critical parts have the greatest combined supply-chain risk?
5. Are particular suppliers, part families or sites responsible for a disproportionate number of quality incidents?
